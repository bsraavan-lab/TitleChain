# TitleChain — Launch Video Narration

*Voice-over script · ~2 minutes · continuous narration*

---

Every year in Tamil Nadu, people buy property that was never safe to buy. Not because the warning wasn't there. It was there, on page six of a document called the Encumbrance Certificate, written in Tamil legal language that almost nobody can read. An old mortgage that was never closed. A seller who never had the right to sell. One line you can't read can cost you everything you've saved.

This is the problem TitleChain was built for: an AI platform that reads the Encumbrance Certificate and turns it into plain English, with proof behind every claim.

Take Priya. She lives in Chicago, and she's buying a house in Coimbatore for her parents. She has never read a Tamil legal document in her life. She uploads the EC to TitleChain, and in about a minute it has read the whole thing.

The legal Tamil becomes plain English. The ownership history becomes a timeline she can scroll: who owned the land, who sold it, and when. Every mortgage and lien is labeled. And the one thing that should worry her is flagged in red: a loan from 2011 with no record of release.

Instead of forty pages of legal text, she sees one dashboard. She types, "Is this loan still active?" and the answer points to the exact line on the original document. She can check the evidence herself. So she calls her lawyer with one specific question, instead of a stack of pages and a prayer.

That's TitleChain. Understanding your property shouldn't depend on the language it was written in. It should be a right, not a privilege.
