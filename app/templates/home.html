What you get back

  • Know what this certificate leaves out

    Which years it covers, which it does not, and every earlier document it points at but nobody has read. All on one screen, in plain words.

  • Turn a gap into one errand

    When something is missing, the request arrives already filled in: sub-registrar office, village, survey numbers, dates. Copy it, paste it into TNREGINET, and drop the certificate back here when it turns up.

  • Hand over a record, not a promise

    One report carries the checks that ran, the ones that could not, and whatever you noted along the way. It goes in the file, and it goes to the bank.

{# Trust, stated as three things we refuse to do. Vaguer reassurance would read as marketing, which is the one thing this screen cannot afford. #}

Why you can rely on it

  • Nothing is guessed

    Findings come from fixed checks written in code, not from a language model's opinion. The same certificate gives you the same answer every time you open it.

  • Every finding shows its source

    Click any line and the exact region of the scanned page appears beside it. If we cannot show you where something came from, we do not claim it.

  • It admits what it could not read

    If we could not make out a date, it stays blank and waits for you to fill it in. A check that could not run says so, and says why. Nothing here quietly counts as a pass.

TitleChain shows you what a certificate does and does not evidence. It does not give an opinion on title. That judgement stays yours.
